M&A evaluation, with a sentiment layer

Is this studio worth acquiring — and what does the world currently think of it?

What is this?

M&A — mergers and acquisitions — is one company buying another. Evaluation is the homework on a candidate studio: is it a good business, what is it worth, what could go wrong. Nobody knows a studio's future, so finance brackets the price from three directions:

Each method gives a range, not an answer — the deal gets negotiated where the ranges overlap. The sentiment layer below decides where in that band you land, or whether to move at all.

The question

Should this studio be on our shortlist — and in what price range?

Today: a Wall-Street-style tool produces a templated profile, then hours of reading news and filings by hand to form a view.

The valuation math, in three lines

Comparables

What similar studios are valued at today, e.g. 4–6× revenue.

Precedent deals

What acquirers actually paid for studios like this one.

DCF

Project the future cash flows, discount them back to today.
