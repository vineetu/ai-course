Revenue concentration analysis

Where does the money actually come from — and how much of it rides on the top few titles?

What is this?

Revenue concentration asks one thing: how much of the money depends on how few titles?

Same total revenue — very different risk. The analysis measures which of these you are, and which way it is drifting quarter over quarter. (Blue = top 10 titles, grey = everything else.)

The question

Is revenue isolated to a handful of AAA games, or is it concentrating in a specific area?

You already have the data — purchased market reports and internal flat files. Today the answer is assembled by hand in Excel.

The workflow

Files in
Market reports (PDF)
Sales flat files (CSV / Excel)
The agent works
  1. reads the files
  2. groups by title, genre, region
  3. ranks & computes shares
  4. drafts the memo
Artifacts out
Chart + one-page memo
A reusable skill — re-run next quarter in minutes

What the answer looks like

Three numbers that measure it

Top-N share

How much of total revenue the biggest N titles hold.

HHI

Sum of squared shares. Below 1,500: spread out. Above 2,500: concentrated.

The trend

The same number, tracked quarter over quarter. Direction matters more than level.
